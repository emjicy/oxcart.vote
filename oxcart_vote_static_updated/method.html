Legitimacy as a Function

Lj(t) = Lj(0) + ∑L’j(t)

This formula expresses the legitimacy of an agent over time based on starting legitimacy and ongoing delegated trust. Voting capital flows through delegation chains, and decisions reflect cumulative support.

Key Concepts

  • Liquid Representation: Agents can change their vote or delegate at any time.
  • Cascading Capital: Unused votes cascade along delegation paths until used.
  • Legitimacy Recalibration: Delegation is reflexive and adjusts based on trust and outcomes.
