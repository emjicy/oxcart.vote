The Oxcart Method is a governance framework designed for DAOs and decentralized systems, enabling real-time recalibration of authority through dynamic delegation. Built by mel.eth, the method allows continuous agent participation and mathematically models legitimacy as a finite, renewable resource.

At its core, Oxcart promotes:

  • 📊 Cascading vote capital accounting
  • 🔁 Recursive delegation chains
  • 📉 Legitimacy depletion & replenishment
  • 🔓 Open, constraint-optional participation

🧠 Inspired by democratic fluidity and liquid representation.
