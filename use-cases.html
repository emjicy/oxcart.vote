✅ Arbitrum: Oxcart Delegation Engine (Live)
Successfully deployed to allow backup delegates, reduce voter fatigue, and enable reflexive delegation flows. Voters could reassign trust dynamically, with onchain visibility into legitimacy shifts.

🔁 Recursive Delegation Chains
Visual simulations and internal testing are underway to model cascading trust flows and network-level trust loops. Ideal for use in any DAO or protocol requiring re-delegation resilience.

Prototypes explore how trust depletes over time and distance in delegation chains. Useful for setting thresholds, triggering backups, or auto-revoking dormant reps.
